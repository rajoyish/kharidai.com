# Search results are unbounded crawl space — every term invents a new URL — so
# they stay blocked. Pagination is not: `?page=2` and beyond are the only route
# to products past the first page of a category, so it must stay crawlable.
# Google applies the most specific rule, so `Allow` wins for paginated URLs.
Disallow: /*?*search=
Allow: /*?*page=
